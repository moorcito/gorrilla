# gorrilla

Web app to share out the parking spots that their owners leave free.

Employees request the days they need a spot. When there are more requests
than free spots, a weekly allocation gives priority to whoever has parked
there least in recent months, and the rest go on a waiting list.

In Spain, a *gorrilla* is the self-appointed attendant who waves drivers into
free parking spots.

**Status:** early development.
