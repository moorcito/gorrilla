# gorrilla

**English** · [Español](README.es.md)

A web app for a community to share its parking spots the way teams share hot
desks: you book a spot for the days you need it.

A spot can belong to someone, who gets first say: it only joins the shared
pool on the days they won't use it.

When there are more requests than free spots, a weekly allocation gives
priority to whoever has parked least in recent months. The rest go on a
waiting list, and the first in line gets the next spot that is freed.

In Spain, a *gorrilla* is the self-appointed attendant who waves drivers into
free parking spots.
